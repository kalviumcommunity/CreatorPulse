# CreatorPulse — Metric Definitions

> All metrics are computed dynamically from the dataset. No values are hardcoded.

## Observed Metrics (directly from data)

### Total Revenue
- **Formula:** `SUM(purchases.order_value)`
- **Classification:** Observed
- **Business Interpretation:** Total monetary value of all orders in the dataset
- **Limitations:** Includes refunds (negative values); does not account for returns processed outside this dataset

### Total Orders
- **Formula:** `COUNT(DISTINCT purchases.order_id)`
- **Classification:** Observed
- **Business Interpretation:** Total number of unique orders placed
- **Limitations:** Deduplication by order_id; some orders may appear as duplicates in raw data

### Unique Customers
- **Formula:** `COUNT(DISTINCT purchases.customer_id)`
- **Classification:** Observed
- **Business Interpretation:** Total distinct customers who made at least one purchase

### New Customers
- **Formula:** `COUNT(DISTINCT customers.customer_id)`
- **Classification:** Observed
- **Business Interpretation:** Total customers acquired through creator referrals

## Derived Metrics (calculated from observed data)

### Repeat Customers
- **Formula:** Count of customer_id WHERE purchase_count > 1
- **Denominator:** N/A
- **Classification:** Derived
- **Business Interpretation:** Customers who made 2+ purchases — indicates customer retention

### Repeat Purchase Rate
- **Formula:** `repeat_customers / unique_customers × 100`
- **Denominator:** unique_customers
- **Classification:** Derived
- **Business Interpretation:** Percentage of customers who return to purchase again
- **Limitations:** Based on observed purchase history only; true repeat rate may differ

### Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Formula:** `total_revenue / total_orders`
- **Denominator:** total_orders
- **Classification:** Derived
- **Business Interpretation:** Average monetary value per order

### Engagement Rate
- **Formula:** `(likes + comments + shares + saves) / impressions × 100`
- **Denominator:** impressions
- **Classification:** Derived
- **Business Interpretation:** Percentage of impressions that resulted in active engagement
- **Limitations:** Does not weight engagement types differently

### Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- **Formula:** `referral_clicks / impressions × 100`
- **Denominator:** impressions
- **Classification:** Derived
- **Business Interpretation:** Percentage of impressions that led to a referral click

### Conversion Rate
- **Formula:** `total_purchases / referral_clicks × 100`
- **Denominator:** referral_clicks (not impressions, not landing_page_visits)
- **Classification:** Derived
- **Business Interpretation:** Percentage of referral clicks that converted to purchases
- **Limitations:** Denominator is referral_clicks at campaign level; per-creator conversion may exceed 100% due to attribution model differences

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- **Formula:** `SUM(campaign_budget) / new_customers`
- **Denominator:** new_customers
- **Classification:** Derived
- **Business Interpretation:** Average cost to acquire one customer
- **Limitations:** Only available because campaign_budget exists in the demo data

### Revenue per Customer
- **Formula:** `total_revenue / unique_customers`
- **Denominator:** unique_customers
- **Classification:** Derived
- **Business Interpretation:** Average observed revenue generated per customer

### 30-Day Repeat Rate
- **Formula:** `customers_purchasing_within_30_days_of_first / first_purchase_customers × 100`
- **Denominator:** customers with a first purchase
- **Classification:** Derived
- **Business Interpretation:** Early retention signal
- **Limitations:** Requires sufficient date range in the data

### Observed Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- **Formula:** `SUM(order_value) per customer_id`
- **Classification:** Observed (not predictive)
- **Business Interpretation:** Total revenue generated by a customer in the observed period
- **Limitations:** This is NOT a predictive LTV model; it only reflects historical spending
